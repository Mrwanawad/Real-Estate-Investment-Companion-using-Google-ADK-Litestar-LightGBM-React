# Reservation and Cancellation Policy (Nile Crest Developments, synthetic)

## Reservation deposit
A buyer reserves a unit by paying a refundable reservation deposit of EGP 100,000 for units priced below EGP 5,000,000, and EGP 200,000 for units at or above that price. The unit is held off the market for 14 calendar days.

## Converting the reservation into a contract
Within the 14 days the buyer must sign the sales contract and pay the down payment. The reservation deposit is then credited against the down payment. If the buyer does not sign in time, the hold expires and the deposit is refunded minus a 2,500 EGP administrative fee.

## Cooling-off period
The buyer may cancel within 7 days of paying the deposit for any reason. The deposit is refunded in full minus the 2,500 EGP administrative fee, within 14 working days, to the original payment method.

## Cancellation after signing
After the contract is signed, a buyer who cancels forfeits 10% of the unit price, or all amounts paid if they are lower. The remainder is refunded in 60 days or when the unit is resold, whichever comes first. Cancellation requests must be made in writing through the sales portal.

## Developer cancellation
If the developer cancels or cannot deliver the reserved unit, the buyer receives a full refund and a 5% goodwill payment on the amounts paid.
