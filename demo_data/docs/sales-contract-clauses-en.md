# Sales Contract Key Clauses (Nile Crest Developments, synthetic)

## Unit description and area
The contract states the unit code, the built-up area (BUA) in square metres, the floor, the finishing level (core and shell, semi-finished or fully finished) and the attached floor plan. A tolerance of 3% in the final built area is accepted. Beyond 3% the price is adjusted at the contract rate per square metre.

## Price and price fixing
The price in EGP is fixed for the whole payment plan. The developer may not raise the price after signing, even if construction costs rise. Extras such as a parking space or a garden are priced separately and listed in an annex.

## Payment schedule
The schedule annex lists the down payment, each instalment date and the final instalment. Payments are made by cheque or bank transfer to the project escrow account.

## Delivery date
The contract states a delivery date and a grace period of 6 months. Delays beyond the grace period trigger compensation as set in the delivery delay policy.

## Buyer obligations
The buyer pays instalments on time, accepts the unit within 30 days of the handover notice and pays the maintenance deposit at handover.

## Dispute resolution
Disputes go first to the developer customer care team, then to arbitration seated in Cairo. Egyptian law governs the contract.
