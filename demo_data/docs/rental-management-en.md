# Rental Management Service (Al Waha Property Management, synthetic)

## Services
Al Waha, a synthetic property manager, markets the unit, screens tenants, signs the lease, collects rent, handles repairs and sends a monthly statement to the owner.

## Fees
The management fee is 8% of collected rent. Finding a new tenant costs one half month of rent. Repairs above EGP 2,000 need owner approval.

## Lease terms
Standard residential leases run 12 months with a deposit of two months rent. Rent can increase 10% at renewal, according to the lease.

## Expected yield
For planning, the manager quotes a gross yield of 6% to 8% of the unit value per year in new compounds, before fees, vacancy and the maintenance service fee. Net yield is usually 1.5 to 2 points lower. These figures are synthetic and not a promise.

## Vacancy
Planning assumes one vacant month per year. Furnished units rent faster but cost more to equip.

## Payment to owner
Net rent is transferred to the owner by the 10th of each month.
