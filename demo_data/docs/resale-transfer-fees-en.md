# Resale and Transfer Fees (Nile Crest Developments, synthetic)

## When a unit can be resold
A buyer may resell the contract once 30% of the price has been paid, with the developer written approval. Before that threshold resale is allowed only in cases of hardship.

## Developer transfer fee
The developer charges an assignment fee of 2% of the contract price when the contract is transferred to a new buyer. The fee is paid by the seller unless the parties agree otherwise. The new buyer takes over the remaining instalments on the same schedule.

## Government and notary costs
Contract registration costs the new owner a fee of 0.5% of the unit value, plus notary and stamp costs of about EGP 3,000. These are separate from the developer fee.

## Resale through the developer
If the buyer asks the developer to market the unit, a brokerage commission of 2.5% of the sale price applies, charged to the seller.

## Capital gains note
This synthetic policy does not give tax advice. Buyers should ask a licensed adviser about taxes on any gain from reselling.
