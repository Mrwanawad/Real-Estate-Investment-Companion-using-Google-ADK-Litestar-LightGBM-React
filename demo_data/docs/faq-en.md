# Buyer FAQ (synthetic)

## Can foreigners buy property in Egypt?
In this synthetic FAQ, foreign buyers may own up to two units, each up to 4,000 square metres of land area, for residential use. Payment is made in foreign currency through a bank.

## Is a larger down payment better?
A larger down payment usually lowers the price and the total paid, but it reduces cash available for emergencies. Compare financing options by present value.

## What is built-up area (BUA)?
BUA is the area of the unit including internal walls, balconies and its share of structure, but excluding shared corridors. Price per square metre is the price divided by BUA.

## What does finishing level mean?
Core and shell means bare walls and floors. Semi-finished adds plaster and basic utilities. Fully finished adds flooring, paint, kitchen and bathrooms.

## Is this tool financial advice?
No. The companion uses synthetic data for demonstration. Real decisions need real offers, a lawyer and an independent adviser.
