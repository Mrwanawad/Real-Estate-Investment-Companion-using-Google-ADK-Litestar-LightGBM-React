# Delivery Delay Compensation (Nile Crest Developments, synthetic)

## Grace period
The contract delivery date includes a grace period of 6 months. No compensation is due inside the grace period.

## Compensation after the grace period
After the grace period the developer pays compensation of 0.5% of the amounts paid by the buyer for every full month of delay, up to a maximum of 12% of the amounts paid. Compensation is deducted from the next instalments or paid in cash on request.

## Long delays
If delivery is delayed by more than 24 months beyond the contract date the buyer may cancel and receive all amounts paid plus 8% yearly simple interest from each payment date.

## Force majeure
Delays caused by force majeure, such as war, natural disaster or a government stop-work order, extend the delivery date and do not trigger compensation. The developer must notify the buyer in writing within 30 days.

## Claim process
The buyer submits a delay claim in the sales portal. The developer answers in 30 days.
