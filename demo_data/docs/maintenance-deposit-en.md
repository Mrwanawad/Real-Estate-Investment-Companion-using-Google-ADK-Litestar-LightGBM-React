# Maintenance Deposit (Nile Crest Developments, synthetic)

## What it is
The maintenance deposit funds the owners association that runs common areas: security, landscaping, lifts, cleaning and the shared utilities. It is a one-time payment collected at handover, separate from the unit price.

## Amount
The deposit is 8% of the unit price for apartments and 10% for villas and townhouses. For a unit priced at EGP 4,000,000 this gives EGP 320,000. The amount is stated in the contract.

## Annual service fee
After handover owners pay an annual service fee of about EGP 120 per square metre of built area, adjusted yearly by inflation up to a 10% cap. The fee is billed quarterly.

## Management and refunds
The deposit is held by an independent account managed by the owners association. It is not refundable on sale; it transfers to the new owner. Annual audited accounts are shared with owners.

## Unpaid fees
Owners who do not pay service fees for six months lose access to shared facilities such as the pool and the gym until the balance is settled.
