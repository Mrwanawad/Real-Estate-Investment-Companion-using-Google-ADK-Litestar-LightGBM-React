# Mortgage Financing Options (synthetic)

## Central bank style subsidised initiative
This synthetic initiative mimics central bank programmes for middle income buyers. It offers a fixed annual rate of 8% for up to 20 years on units with a price below EGP 2,500,000. The buyer household income must be below EGP 45,000 per month and the buyer must not own another home.

## Commercial mortgage
First Pyramid Bank, a synthetic bank, offers a variable rate equal to the corridor rate plus 2%, currently about 25% per year, for 5 to 15 years. Loan to value is up to 80% of the unit price. The bank requires the unit to be registered or the developer to give a payment guarantee.

## Eligibility and affordability
The monthly payment must not exceed 40% of documented net income. The bank asks for six months of salary statements, the national ID, and the unit valuation done by an approved surveyor.

## Fees
Typical costs are 1% to 1.5% of the loan as an arrangement fee, a valuation fee of EGP 5,000, and life insurance charged yearly. Early repayment costs 2% of the repaid amount in the first 3 years.

## Developer instalments versus mortgage
Developer instalments have no interest in the nominal price but the price is higher. A mortgage lowers the cash needed today but the total interest can be large. Always compare present values with the same discount rate.
