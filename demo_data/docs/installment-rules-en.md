# Installment Plan Rules (Nile Crest Developments, synthetic)

## Plan structure
Plans run 5 to 9 years. The buyer pays a down payment of 10% to 20% of the price, then equal quarterly instalments. A larger down payment or a shorter plan lowers the total price. Paying the full balance early earns a 6% annual discount on the remaining nominal amount.

## Late fees
An instalment is late after a 15 day grace period. After that a late fee of 1.5% per month is charged on the overdue amount, counted per started month. Three consecutive late instalments allow the developer to issue a formal notice, and after 60 more days to cancel the contract under the cancellation policy.

## Price escalation
The standard plan has no price escalation: the price is fixed at signing. Some promotional plans add an annual escalation of 5% on unpaid instalments, which is written in the schedule annex. Buyers should compare the present value of such plans, not only the total nominal amount.

## Rescheduling
A buyer facing hardship may ask once per year to move up to two instalments by 90 days. The request costs a 1% administrative fee on the moved amount.

## Final instalment
Some plans defer a balloon payment of 10% to handover. The buyer should plan the cash for it early.
